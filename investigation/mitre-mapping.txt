PHISHING EMAIL - MITRE ATT&CK MAPPING
======================================

Technique:
T1566 - Phishing

Sub-technique:
T1566.002 - Phishing: Spearphishing Link


OBSERVED BEHAVIOUR
------------------

The simulated email contains a link directing the recipient to
a supposed Microsoft account verification page.

The message uses urgent account-suspension language to encourage
the recipient to follow the link.


EVIDENCE
--------

Suspicious URL:
https://login-microsoft-security.example/verify

Subject:
URGENT: Your Microsoft 365 account will be suspended

Email authentication:
SPF: FAIL
DKIM: FAIL
DMARC: FAIL


RATIONALE
---------

T1566.002 is an appropriate mapping because the simulated
phishing scenario attempts to persuade the recipient to interact
with a suspicious link delivered through email.


ANALYST NOTE
------------

This is a mapping of simulated behaviour to the MITRE ATT&CK
framework. It does not represent evidence of a real-world
attacker or campaign.
