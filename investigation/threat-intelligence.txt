an IOC has previously been associated with malicious activity.

REAL SOC WORKFLOW
=================

For a real phishing incident, a SOC analyst could investigate
the extracted indicators using approved threat-intelligence
sources.

Possible checks include:

- Domain reputation
- IP reputation
- URL reputation
- WHOIS/domain registration information
- Passive DNS information
- Malware or phishing reports
- Previous internal security alerts
- Vendor threat-intelligence reports

The analyst should record the source, date checked, result and
confidence level.

Analysts should not automatically classify an IOC as malicious
without sufficient supporting evidence.


THREAT INTELLIGENCE CONCLUSION
==============================

The simulated indicators cannot provide a genuine real-world
reputation verdict.

However, the email already contains several independent phishing
indicators, including a lookalike sender domain, urgent
social-engineering language, a suspicious verification URL and
failed email authentication checks.

Further investigation would be appropriate in a real SOC
environment.
